# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Every employee receives 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to
five unused days may be carried over into the next year, and they must be used
by the end of March.

## Sickness

Sickness absence is reported to the line manager before 9:30 on the first day.
A medical certificate is needed for absences longer than three working days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Requests are made
in the HR portal at least eight weeks before the planned start date.
