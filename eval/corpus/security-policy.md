# Security policy

## Access reviews

All production access is reviewed every quarter. The reviews are signed off by
the security lead, currently Tomas Berg. Access that is not re-approved within
ten working days is revoked automatically.

## Passwords and accounts

Passwords must be at least fourteen characters long. Shared accounts are not
allowed; each person uses a vault entry of their own. Administrators must use
a hardware security key for every sign-in to production.

## Incidents

Suspected incidents are reported to the security mailbox within 24 hours of
discovery. The security lead opens an incident record and assigns an owner the
same day. Customer notifications follow the legal review, never before it.
