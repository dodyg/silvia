# Product FAQ

## Data retention

Application logs are kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Customer
data is kept for as long as the account is active, and for 30 days after it is
closed so that it can be restored.

## Exports

Customers can export their data as CSV or as JSON from the settings page. Large
exports are emailed as a download link that expires after seven days.

## Plans and sign-in

The Basic plan costs 9 euros per user per month. The Team plan costs 29 euros
per user per month and adds single sign-on through SAML. Offline mode is not
available in any plan.
