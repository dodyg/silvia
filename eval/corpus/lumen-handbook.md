# Lumen field handbook

## Launch schedule

The Lumen pilot launches on 14 March 2027 in two cities: Lisbon and Nairobi.
The launch owner is Ines Carvalho, who reports to the operations director.
Training for field leads runs on 2 February 2027 at the Lisbon depot.

## Equipment

Each field team carries a sealed Lumen kit with a serial prefix of LK-.
The kit includes a solar charger rated for 40 watts and a spare battery pack
of 20 amp hours. A team has four people, and each person signs for one kit.

## Returns

Damaged kits go back to the depot within five working days. The depot
replaces a damaged charger within two days of receiving it.
