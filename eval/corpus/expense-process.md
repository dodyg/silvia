# Expense process

## Submitting claims

Expense reports are submitted once a month, by the fifth working day. Claims
above 500 euros need sign-off from the chief financial officer before payment.

## Mileage

Business travel by private car is reimbursed at 0.42 euros per kilometre. The
journey log must show the start point, the destination and the purpose.

## Receipts

A photo of a receipt is accepted for up to 30 days after the purchase. After
that, the original receipt is required, and the claim may be declined.
