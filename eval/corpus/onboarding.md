# New joiner onboarding

## Day one

Your laptop is ready at the IT desk on the second floor. Collect your badge at
reception on level two, then meet your buddy, who is assigned in the HR portal
before your start date.

## First week

Request access to the source repositories with the access request form. Access
is usually granted within one working day. Complete the security training in
the learning portal before you open any production system.

## Checklist

The onboarding checklist is in the HR portal under your name. Your manager signs
it off at the end of the first week.
