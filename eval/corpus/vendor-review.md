# Vendor review

## Annual review

Every vendor that handles company data is reviewed once every 12 months. The
review uses an 80-question security questionnaire, and the answers are checked
against the vendor's own evidence.

## Critical vendors

Critical vendors must provide a SOC 2 Type II report, and that report must be
no more than twelve months old. A vendor without a current report cannot be
onboarded until the gap is closed.

## Approval

New vendors are approved by the procurement lead, Dana Okafor, after the
security review passes. Contracts are signed only after that approval.
