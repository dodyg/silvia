# Travel policy

## Booking

Flights for business trips are booked through the travel desk. Flights shorter
than six hours are booked in economy class. Longer flights may be booked in
premium economy with approval from the line manager.

## Hotels and allowances

Hotel bookings must stay under the nightly cap of 180 euros unless the trip has
been approved by the operations director in advance. The daily meal allowance
is 45 euros for domestic trips and 60 euros abroad.

## Receipts

Taxi and transport receipts are reimbursed monthly when submitted with the
expense report. Receipts must show the date, the amount and the supplier name.
