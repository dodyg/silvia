# Travel policy (2025 edition)

This edition was superseded on 1 January 2027 by the current travel policy. It
is kept for reference only, for claims about trips taken before that date.

## Booking

Flights for business trips are booked through the travel desk or, for trips
inside the country, directly by the traveller. Flights shorter than eight hours
are booked in economy class.

## Hotels and allowances

Hotel bookings must stay under the nightly cap of 150 euros. The daily meal
allowance is 40 euros for domestic trips and 50 euros abroad.

## Receipts

Transport receipts are reimbursed every quarter when submitted with the
expense report.
